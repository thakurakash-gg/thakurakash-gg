About the studio

Different disciplines.
A shared way of working.

Kazeyami Innovations is a design and technology studio based in Lucknow. Our artists and developers work across 3D visualization, animation, branding and digital products.

We can build an architectural scene, shape a character or work through an interface. The medium changes. The care we put into it stays the same.
