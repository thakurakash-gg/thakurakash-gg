NEXIV is Kazeyami’s architecture and visualization branch. RAY is our interactive platform within NEXIV. The public waterfront prototype lets you orbit an exterior scene, zoom, choose camera views and switch between day and night. Project-specific interiors, unit selection and material configuration can be discussed as separate deliverables.

01 / EXPLORE

Understand the setting.

Orbit the towers and inspect the waterfront, marina and surrounding neighborhood. Use the camera presets to compare different exterior views.

02 / COMPARE

See the light change.

Switch between morning, afternoon, evening and night. Review how the architecture and landscape read in each lighting condition.

03 / CUSTOM BUILD

Scope the next interaction.

For a commissioned experience, we agree the content, controls, device targets and delivery method. Interior walkthroughs and configurators are not part of this public exterior demo.

SERVICE CATALOGUE / DELIVERABLES & BRIEFS
01 / SERVICE / NEXIV

Architectural visualization

Present the space. Explain the design.

For architects, developers and property teams presenting a proposal, a development or a launch.

  • Interior and exterior still renders
  • Architectural walkthroughs and cinematic films
  • Masterplan, landscape and aerial visualization
  • 360° panoramas and connected virtual tours

What to send us

Floor plans, elevations or a model; material and landscape references; required views and intended use.

What you receive

Agreed-resolution images, edited films or linked panoramas. Camera list, duration, aspect ratios and review stages are set in the quotation.

Architectural real-time, VR & AR

Let people explore and choose.

For architectural sales galleries, property presentations and spatial demonstrations that need more than a fixed camera.

  • Unreal Engine architectural experiences
  • Browser-based architectural experiences
  • VR and AR experiences
  • Architectural material and finish interactions

What to send us

A scoped interactive build with agreed controls, content and device testing. Hosting, deployment and ongoing support are specified separately.

03 / SERVICE / NEXIV

CAD, BIM & design development

Make the design easier to coordinate.

For architectural teams developing a design, organizing technical information or preparing it for visualization.
