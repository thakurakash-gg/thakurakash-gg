Blue Hour is an original stage and lighting design study imagined for Ado, set in the geographic context of Shiokaze Park, Odaiba, Tokyo.

This is an independent, uncommissioned concept with no artist or venue affiliation. The event layout, stage and show visuals are proposed designs. Surroundings are simplified from mapped geography, not a site survey.

Explore: drag to orbit; scroll or pinch to zoom. Use two fingers or right-drag to move the view. With the scene focused, use arrow keys to orbit, + / − to zoom and Home to reset.

Show desk: try lighting cues and fixture groups. DMX values and fireworks are visual simulations. No audio, artist recordings or live equipment control is included.

Reduced-motion settings pause continuous lighting movement and replace fireworks with a static finale look.
