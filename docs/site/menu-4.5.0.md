# Menu background 4.5.0

Replaces the temple vignette with an original folded-metal sculpture: three parametric ribbons in champagne, silver and muted copper, lit by a locally generated studio environment. A dark scrim keeps the navigation text clear. The links, panel width, right-side slide, focus trap, Escape close and theme controls are retained.

The scene has three mesh draws and 6,912 triangles, without external models or images. It creates the renderer only on first open, caps DPR at 1.25 and frame rate at 24 fps, pauses when closed or hidden, and renders a still when reduced motion is requested. Pointer movement adds restrained parallax. The existing local Three.js runtime is reused or loaded on demand for subpages.

Validation: source syntax and marketing asset/identity checks, desktop and 390-pixel phone inspection, no horizontal overflow, readable links, successful Escape close and no browser console errors. Physical low-end phone GPU performance has not been measured. The hero, katana entrance and RAY scene are unchanged.
