## Practical limits

These are browser viewport checks and source/resource tests, not a physical-device performance benchmark. Real phone speed still depends on hardware, browser and network. The RAY experience is a separate, heavier 3D destination; its existing adaptive quality system remains in place.

The enquiry form prepares a local email draft. The visitor must review and send it using an email app, or copy the brief and send it directly. There is no server-side inbox submission, delivery confirmation or CRM integration. The page states this clearly; it does not display a false “sent” confirmation.

No contact email delivery, client references, private case-study permissions or app-store releases were verified. A future server-backed form requires an agreed submission endpoint and delivery setup.
