04 / SERVICE / KAZEYAMI STUDIO

3D assets, characters & animation

Build the form, movement and detail.

For games, products, films and explainers that need assets or complete animated sequences.

  • Character, prop and environment modelling
  • Digital sculpting, retopology, UVs and texturing
  • Rigging, character animation and game cinematics
  • Product renders, turntables and animation

What to send us

Concepts or references, dimensions or scale, art direction, target engine and technical budgets.

What you receive

Agreed models, textures, rigs or rendered sequences. File formats, polygon budgets, texture sizes and source-file handover are confirmed in scope.

Medical & scientific visualization

Make complex subjects understandable.

  • Medical explainers and procedure animation
  • Storyboards, labels and visual sequences
