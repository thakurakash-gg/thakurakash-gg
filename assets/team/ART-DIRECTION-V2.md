# Kazeyami crew wall — original comic cast

Created 20 October 2026 with the built-in image-generation tool. These are original fictional identities for the studio team, not likeness portraits. No existing game or anime characters were supplied as references.

Final asset: `kazeyami-crew-v2.webp`, 1536 × 1024, 4 × 2 transparent atlas, 362,448 bytes. The seven cells follow Siya, Pragati, DK, Deepraj, Shayan, Akash, Hammer; the final cell is empty. The full PNG is archived at `outputs/studio-team-art/kazeyami-crew-comic-v2.png` in the local project.

## Research and design interpretation

- [Massive Monster](https://massivemonster.com/about): expressive ink portraits and individual coloured backings, with literal names and roles.
- [Double Fine](https://www.doublefine.com/about/team): a playful visual identity and emphasis on people and group culture.
- [Supergiant](https://www.supergiantgames.com/team/): clear names, disciplines and human contribution.

Kazeyami's adaptation uses a shared illustrated crew wall, a different colour per teammate, compact real role descriptions and a restrained printed-paper background. It does not copy any studio's illustrations or fabricate personal biographies, statistics or credentials.
