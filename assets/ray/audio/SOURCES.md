# Coastal sound sources

Both recordings are CC0 1.0, as confirmed on the source pages on 2026-10-19.

- `coast-gulls.mp3`: three trimmed 3-second excerpts (2–5, 12–15, 25–28 seconds) from [Seagull on beach](https://freesound.org/people/squashy555/sounds/353416/) by squashy555. Source preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/353/353416_1149179-hq.mp3
- `coast-shore.mp3`: two trimmed 5-second excerpts (2–7, 16–21 seconds) from [Gentle Waves - Quiet Beach 2](https://freesound.org/people/amholma/sounds/376796/) by Andrew Holman / amholma, recorded in Destin, Florida. Source preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/376/376796_6128004-hq.mp3

Adaptations: mono 22.05 kHz / 48 kbps MP3, wind/ultrasonic filtering, loudness normalization to -22 LUFS, short fades. Playback gain stays low. Total compressed audio: 115,182 bytes. Cues are not looped; independent random gaps of 18–45 seconds follow each cue. Gull calls are less frequent in night mode. These are coastal ambience recordings, not claimed to have been recorded in Miami.
