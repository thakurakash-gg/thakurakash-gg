# Coastal sound sources

All three recordings are CC0 1.0, as confirmed on the source pages on 2026-10-19.

- `coast-gulls.mp3`: three trimmed 3-second excerpts (2–5, 12–15, 25–28 seconds) from [Seagull on beach](https://freesound.org/people/squashy555/sounds/353416/) by squashy555. Source preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/353/353416_1149179-hq.mp3
- `coast-shore.mp3`: two trimmed 5-second excerpts (2–7, 16–21 seconds) from [Gentle Waves - Quiet Beach 2](https://freesound.org/people/amholma/sounds/376796/) by Andrew Holman / amholma, recorded in Destin, Florida. Source preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/376/376796_6128004-hq.mp3

- `coast-wind.mp3`: an 8-second excerpt (0.5–8.5 seconds) from [Gentle wind](https://freesound.org/people/fthgurdy/sounds/528944/) by fthgurdy. Source preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/528/528944_3302313-hq.mp3

Adaptations: mono 22.05 kHz / 48 kbps MP3, filtering, loudness normalization to -22 LUFS, short fades. Total compressed audio: 163,369 bytes. Audio stays opt-in and unlooped. Wave gain follows camera distance to water, bird calls increase with height and wind gusts increase with exposure. Short natural gaps separate the cues; a change between waterfront and elevated views fades out the old cue and starts the new one promptly. Gull calls are quieter at night. These are illustrative ambience recordings, not claimed to have been recorded in Miami.
