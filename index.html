BEFORE WE BEGINA FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Let’s make it clear.

What should I include in my project brief?+

Tell us about the project, its audience, and how you plan to use the work. Include plans or models if available, visual references, the deliverables you need, and your target date.

Which visualization format should I choose?+

Still images communicate key views. Films show a space through motion and narrative. A 360° tour connects fixed viewpoints, while the public RAY prototype offers exterior orbit, zoom and day/night views. Other interactive features are scoped for each project.

How are pricing and timelines decided?+

They depend on the scope, complexity, available source material, and required deliverables. Share your brief so we can discuss an appropriate quotation and schedule.

Can I discuss design, branding, or technology work?+

Yes. Kazeyami Studio covers 2D and 3D creative work; NEXIV covers architecture and visualization; Products & Ventures covers development; Events & Experiences covers physical production and live events. Share the brief and we will connect the right disciplines.
