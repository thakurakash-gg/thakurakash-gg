BEFORE WE BEGINA FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

A little clarity.

What should I include in my brief?+

Tell us about the project, its audience and how you plan to use the work. Include plans or models if available, references, the deliverables you need and your target date.

Which visualization format fits my project?+

Still images communicate key views. Films show a space through motion. A 360° tour connects fixed viewpoints, while RAY offers an interactive way to explore. We can discuss the right format for your brief.

How do pricing and timelines work?+

They depend on scope, complexity, available source material and required deliverables. Share your brief so we can discuss an appropriate quotation and schedule.

Can my project combine different services?+

Yes. Kazeyami connects design, branding, technology, 3D and architectural visualization. Tell us what you are planning and we can explore the scope together.
