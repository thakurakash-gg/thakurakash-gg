BEFORE WE BEGIN

A little clarity.

What should I include in my brief?+

Tell us about the audience, purpose and intended use. Share plans or models if available, visual references, the deliverables you need and your target date.

Which visualization format fits my project?+

Still images focus on key views. Films reveal a space through motion. A 360° tour connects fixed viewpoints, while RAY offers an interactive way to explore. We can help you choose around your brief.

How do pricing and timelines work?+

Scope, complexity, available source material and deliverables determine the quotation and schedule. Share your brief so we can discuss both.

Can a project combine different services?+

Yes. Our artists and developers work across design, branding, 3D, architecture and technology. The combination follows what your project needs.
