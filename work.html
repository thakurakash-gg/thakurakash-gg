RAY waterfront

Interactive exterior prototype

A waterfront exterior environment built for orbiting, examining the surroundings and comparing the atmosphere at different times of day.

Launch the prototype ↗
What it demonstrates
Real-time scene presentation, browser interaction, camera views, environment lighting and optional coastal audio.
Scope & context
The public version is an exterior prototype. It demonstrates orbit, zoom, view presets and day/night lighting; interior navigation, unit selection and material configuration are not included in this demo.
