FROM THE BRIEF TO THE SHOW FLOOR

One production plan.
Clear responsibilities.

01 / Plan

Share the date, venue, audience, format, budget range, site dimensions and existing equipment. We define the layout, production scope and responsibilities.

02 / Prepare

We agree the equipment list, fabrication requirements, creative content, crew, installation schedule and show cues. Site access and venue requirements are coordinated in the plan.

03 / Operate

The scoped team installs, tests and rehearses the setup, operates the show and coordinates dismantling or permanent handover as agreed.

Each proposal specifies equipment supply or rental, crew, venue access, installation and dismantling, power and rigging responsibilities, required permissions, travel and any third-party costs. The scope is agreed for the venue and event.
